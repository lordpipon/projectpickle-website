The launcher never sees your password.

It asks the backend for a short code, opens this site, and waits. You sign in here, approve the code, and the session lands on your PC.

  1. 01

    Start a sign-in

    The launcher requests a code and opens this page in your browser.

  2. 02

    Approve in the browser

    Sign in or create an account. The code is bound to your user, not to a machine.

  3. 03

    Queue up

    The launcher swaps the code for a session and rotates it while you play.

Codes expire after ten minutes. Refresh tokens are hashed at rest and rotated on every use.

Four modes, one hundred players.

Every lobby is the same size. What changes is how many people you have to trust.

  • Solo Free for all, one life, nobody to blame but the drop. 100 × 1
  • Duo Fifty pairs. Your partner can be revived if you get there in time. 50 × 2
  • Trio Thirty-three teams, with one seat filled by the matchmaker. 33 × 3 + 1
  • Squad Twenty-five squads of four. Rotations matter more than aim. 25 × 4

Stats that start before the match.

Placement, eliminations and damage feed XP. XP sets your starting matchmaking bracket, so the lobby roughly matches the work you have put in.

  • Match historyStored on the server, not on your machine
  • LeaderboardsSorted by XP, wins or eliminations
  • LevelGrows with cumulative XP, never resets

Before you write

Do I need another store or launcher?
No. Project Pickle ships its own launcher, account system and matchmaker.
What if the queue cannot fill?
The skill window widens as you wait, and empty seats are filled by bots after a grace period.
How do I get help?
Visit the support page for email, Discord and contact information.
Is my password stored in the launcher?
No. The launcher only receives a session after you approve a code on this site.
